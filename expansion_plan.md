# "Expand to 10 campuses" rollout plan (edit with your findings)

1. **Lead with the winning channel** from channel_funnel.csv (lowest cost per activated student) and keep one low-cost secondary channel as a hedge.
2. **Message to the top persona** (see personas.csv): lead with their #1 pain point, not product features.
3. **Campus ambassador model:** 1 ambassador per campus, target [X] activated students/month; reward with certificates/LORs and PPO-priority visibility.
4. **Referral loop:** add a "bring a friend" prompt after sprint completion to push K-factor towards > 0.3.
5. **Weekly dashboard:** reach -> sign-up -> apply -> complete per campus; pause campuses below the sign-up % benchmark after 2 weeks.
6. **Pilot first:** run campus #2 and #3 with the same playbook, compare cost per activated student to NIT Agartala baseline before scaling.
